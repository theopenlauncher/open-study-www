Features

Built around how studying actually works.

A few tools, done carefully — each one designed to keep you on the work in front of you.

Subjects & pages

Every subject gets a page you shape yourself.

Each subject starts as a blank page. Add what that course needs — notes, a flashcard deck, a task list, a timer, a quiz — from a single / menu, and move blocks around as the term goes on.

Your whole course lives in one place, instead of across five apps and a folder of screenshots.

Flashcards

Cards come back right before you'd forget them.

Every card is scheduled with the SM-2 spaced-repetition algorithm. Rate each one Again, Hard, Good or Easy and see exactly when it will return. Review with the keyboard, or import an existing deck from CSV or plain text.

Review time goes to the cards you're shaky on, not the ones you already know.

Focus timer

A Pomodoro timer that keeps count for you.

Work in focused sessions with short and long breaks, or switch to a distraction-free Focus mode. Each session is logged against its subject and counts toward your daily goal, streak and statistics.

You can see where your week actually went — without keeping a spreadsheet.

PDF reader

Read and mark up the PDFs you're given.

Lecture slides and papers open in a native PDF reader, inline on a page or in their own window. Highlight passages, add sticky notes, and find them again in the annotations list. Imported files are copied into the app, so they never go missing.

Your readings sit next to the notes and cards they turn into.

And the rest of your week

Tasks
Grouped into Overdue, Today and Upcoming, with priorities, due dates and subjects.
Calendar
Study sessions, exams and deadlines in a month view, with a countdown to every exam.
Quizzes
Multiple choice, true/false and short answer. Retake them and review what you missed.
Habits
Small daily routines with streaks and a 16-week history.
Statistics
Study time, reviews and focus by subject over 7, 30 or 90 days.
Rich notes
Markdown shortcuts, images, tables and code, on native macOS text editing.

Private and open

Your notes stay on your Mac.

  • No account. Open it and start studying.
  • Works offline. No sync service, no internet required.
  • Open source. Read, build and change it under the AGPL-3.0.

Open Study sends a small amount of anonymous usage data by default, and you can turn it off during setup or in Settings. It never includes your notes, card text, task titles, subject names or files. Read the details.
